What happens
in the bay

Four services, no bundles you didn't ask for. Every job starts with a paint reading and a written condition report before a pad ever touches the clear coat.

01 / CORRECTION

Multi-stage polish

Swirls, wash marks and dealer sanding removed under 6000K inspection lighting.

  • Paint depth mapped, 40+ readings
  • Compound, refine, finish
  • Trim & glass decontaminated
02 / COATING

Ceramic protection

9H coatings on paint, wheels, glass and trim. Cured 12 hours in-shop, never outdoors.

  • 2 to 9 year systems
  • Wheels off for barrel coating
  • Annual inspection included
03 / FILM

Paint protection film

Computer-cut XPEL Ultimate Plus on the panels that actually take rock damage.

  • Full front or track package
  • Wrapped edges, no visible lines
  • 10 year film warranty
04 / INTERIOR

Interior reset

Hot-water extraction, leather rehydration, and ozone treatment for smoke or pet odour.

  • Seats removed on request
  • pH-neutral leather care
  • Headliner safe method
05 / MAINTENANCE

Coating maintenance wash

Two-bucket, pH-neutral, touchless dry. Keeps the warranty valid and the gloss honest.

  • Members from $95
  • Every 8 to 10 weeks
  • Saturday drop-off slots
06 / TRACK

Track day prep & return

Pre-event tape-up and post-event rubber, brake dust and bug removal for CTMP regulars.

  • Wheel face & barrel deep clean
  • Iron removal, tar removal
  • Same-week turnaround

Why Apex

Two cars a day.
Never five.

  • 01

    Your car is not on a conveyor

    We book a maximum of two vehicles per day across two bays. That's why a Signature takes three days instead of an afternoon.

  • 02

    Written condition report, before and after

    Photographed defects, paint thickness readings by panel, and a note on anything we recommend leaving alone.

  • 03

    Warranty that we service ourselves

    Coatings are inspected annually in-shop. If gloss or beading falls off inside the term, we re-apply the affected panels at no charge.

    $2M liability, climate-controlled storage, and 24-hour camera coverage. Your keys never leave the building.
