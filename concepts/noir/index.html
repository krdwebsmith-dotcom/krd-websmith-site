The House

Brick, brass,
and no television.

We took over an 1890s storefront on Wellington and kept the tin ceiling. There is a record player, a kettle, and four American-made chairs that were reupholstered by hand in Almonte. What there isn't: a queue, a sports channel, or anyone asking whether you'd like product today.

  • 01
    Appointments run on time. If we're late, your cut is on the house.
  • 02
    Every barber trains one evening a week — classic scissor work, not just fades.
  • 03
    Tools sterilised between every client, in front of you.
  • 04
    Espresso, rye, or nothing at all. No upsell, ever.
