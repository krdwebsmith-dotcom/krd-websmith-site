The thinking

Built around the problem,
not a template.

An editorial barbershop concept designed to feel deliberate and distinct without making booking harder.

The goal is not to make every project look like KRD Websmith. The goal is to make each interface feel native to the business it represents.

01

Challenge

The category is crowded with the same dark masculine aesthetic and interchangeable layouts.

02

Direction

Shift toward editorial typography, warm paper tones, restrained color, and a calmer information hierarchy.

03

Purpose

Make the shop more memorable while keeping service selection and booking straightforward.
